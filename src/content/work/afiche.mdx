afiche answers a small, stubborn question: what's playing in Buenos Aires
cinemas right now, and which of it is worth the trip? The city's listings live
scattered across a dozen theatre sites, each with its own bad calendar — so the
real friction isn't choosing a film, it's just *finding out what's on* before
you give up and stream something at home.

afiche pulls the screens of Buenos Aires into one place and treats them like a
feed worth reading: what's showing, where, and when. The aim is for it to feel
closer to a friend with great taste telling you "there's something worth seeing
this week" than to a showtimes database.

**Status:** Live in production at [afiche.ar](https://afiche.ar) — still evolving.
