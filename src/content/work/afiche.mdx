afiche is about the gap between "I want to watch something good" and actually
finding it. Streaming catalogs are vast and badly indexed; recommendation feeds
optimize for retention, not taste.

I'm building a quieter way in — closer to how a friend with great taste would
hand you a film, than how an algorithm shoves one at you.

**Status:** In progress.
