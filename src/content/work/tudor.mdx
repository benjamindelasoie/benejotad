tudor is a web app for preparing for the Cambridge C1 Advanced (CAE) English
exam. V1 covers two parts of it: Use of English, as objective drills, and
Writing, where an essay comes back graded on the official four-criteria rubric.
It's multi-user and invite-only.

The name is the House of Tudor — 1485 to 1603, the stretch in which modern
English was being forged. English heritage without the stuffiness.

The incumbents here are Cambridge's own Write & Improve and Test & Train:
authoritative, and dated. My bet is that a candidate really only wants one
sentence answered at every moment — *I always know where I stand and exactly
what to fix* — and that answering it well is mostly a design problem.

**Status:** In active development.
