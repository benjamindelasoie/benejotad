camusean is about reading literature in a language you're still learning. The
usual tools break the spell: tap-to-translate yanks you out, parallel texts let
you cheat without noticing, graded readers flatten the prose.

I want the friction in the right place — enough support to keep going, not so
much that you stop reading and start studying.
