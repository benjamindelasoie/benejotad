This is where your first real trip post goes. Pick something with a clear arc and
photos you actually like — recent beats optimal.

A trip post can open with a scene. The morning you arrived, the thing you didn't
expect, the meal you keep thinking about.

## A section

Trip posts support headings, lists, **emphasis**, and [links](/now). Use them to
break a few days into moments rather than a diary.

- a detail
- another detail
- the one that surprised you

When you're ready, this post can link to a food post, a film it reminded you of,
or a project it sparked — and those connections show up in the Related rail.
